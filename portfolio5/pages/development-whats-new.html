What's New in Prog-Net 3.0

Prog-Net 3.0 is a different direction from the earlier generations. Instead of a single launcher with multiple applications, the project focuses on independent software products and a broader development approach.

Documentation pageDetails subject to confirmation

AConfirmed Direction Changes

DirectionConfirmed

Prog-Net 3.0 introduces three independent product concepts: Handmade Marketplace, MicroJobs, and Modern Ticketing System.

i

This describes the stated direction, not a claim that the products are complete or launched.

Compared with earlier generationsConfirmed

Prog-Net 1.0 started with C and Prog-Net 2.0 used Java Swing for games, utilities, and a lobby. Prog-Net 3.0 shifts toward three independent product concepts.
