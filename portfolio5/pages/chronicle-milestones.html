THE CHRONICLE· PROG-NET 3.0

Evolution & Milestones

This timeline is intentionally evidence-led. No dates have been supplied, so milestones remain undated until the project history can be verified.

Documentation pageDetails subject to confirmation

#Project timeline

The project can be understood across three major generations: Prog-Net 1.0, Prog-Net 2.0, and Prog-Net 3.0. The key milestones are the move from C-based applications to Java Swing, the introduction of a centralized lobby, and the shift toward independent software products.

i

The chronology focuses on confirmed changes in project direction rather than guessed dates or unsupported claims.

#Major achievements

The major achievements include the expansion from a small learning experiment to a collection of 19 applications, the growth of a Java Swing environment, and the introduction of four product concepts for Prog-Net 3.0.

i

Achievements should be supported by evidence and updated when verified.

#Important decisions

Key decisions include moving from C to Java, centralizing application navigation through a lobby, and reorganizing the project around independent products instead of a single launcher. These decisions shaped the project’s structure and future direction.

i

Decision records should be linked to source discussions or approved project materials.

#Across the generations

Prog-Net 1.0 focused on practical experimentation and connected applications. Prog-Net 2.0 expanded the project with Java Swing, games, utilities, and more structured navigation. Prog-Net 3.0 introduced multiple product concepts and a more focused software development approach.

i

The journey illustrates a gradual evolution in both technical direction and team thinking.
