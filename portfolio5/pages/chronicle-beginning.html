The Beginning

Prog-Net began as an academic software development project and gradually evolved into a journey spanning multiple generations. What started as an initial exploration of programming and software development became an opportunity to experiment with ideas, build practical applications, and develop technical skills through teamwork.

Documentation pageDetails subject to confirmation

#An Experiment Before a Project

Prog-Net 1.0 did not begin as a conventional software project. It started as a learning-based experiment built around observation, creativity, and ambition. The team studied other student projects, identified ideas with potential, and explored how they could be improved through a different mindset and greater creative effort. Instead of treating existing ideas as limits, the team treated them as starting points. This process became the foundation for Prog-Net.

i

The project began with observation, inspiration, and creative experimentation rather than a fixed product plan.

#Beyond the Ordinary

Our initial objective was not simply to complete a few small applications. We wanted to challenge ourselves, explore different possibilities, and make the most of the time available to us. The goals were to explore multiple software concepts, learn through practical implementation, improve ideas through independent creativity and execution, and make productive use of the full development period.

i

The first goals centered on experimentation, skills development, and collaborative problem-solving.

#Inspiration or Imitation?

Taking inspiration from other teams came with its own challenges. Since some ideas originated from observing projects around us, our work was sometimes labelled as copying. However, inspiration was only the starting point. We reinterpreted those concepts, implemented them independently, and invested considerably more effort into expanding their scope and execution. We chose not to let those opinions define our work.

i

The early project faced criticism and uncertainty, but the team focused on learning, execution, and improvement.
