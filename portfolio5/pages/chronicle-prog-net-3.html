THE CHRONICLE· PROG-NET 3.0

Prog-Net 3.0

Prog-Net 3.0 marks a new chapter in the Prog-Net journey. After exploring C-based applications in the first generation and building a Java Swing application in the second, the third generation shifted its focus towards developing independent software products. Rather than bringing games and utilities together in a single application, Prog-Net 3.0 explored ideas designed to address different needs and audiences.

Documentation pageDetails subject to confirmation

#A New Direction

Prog-Net 3.0 introduced four product concepts: Handmade Marketplace, MicroJobs, Modern Ticketing System, and Mosque Tracker. As the project developed, priorities changed. Handmade Marketplace and Modern Ticketing System remained active, while MicroJobs and Mosque Tracker were placed on hold. This generation represents a broader approach to software development: identifying problems, exploring practical solutions, dividing responsibilities, and turning ideas into working products.

i

The project moved from a single launcher model to a product-based direction.

#The Four Product Concepts

Prog-Net 3.0 introduced four different product ideas, each exploring a separate area of software development. Although they share the same project journey, each concept has its own purpose, intended audience, and potential features. Handmade Marketplace focuses on connecting handmade creators with customers. MicroJobs explores short-term and flexible job opportunities. Modern Ticketing System focuses on structured support requests and technical issue tracking. Mosque Tracker is a concept focused on discovering and accessing mosque-related information.

i

Each product represents a different direction explored during Prog-Net 3.0.

#Development Approach

Unlike previous generations, Prog-Net 3.0 focuses on developing separate software products rather than combining multiple applications into one launcher. Each product has its own purpose, requirements, and development direction. This approach allows the team to focus on the specific problems each product is intended to address while also building broader full-stack skills across the team.

i

Development is organized around product ideas and learning, not around a single integrated app.

#The Road Ahead

Prog-Net 3.0 is a learning-driven development journey. Rather than rushing towards finished products, the focus is on understanding requirements, learning necessary technologies, and building each product through a structured process. As the team develops, priorities may change and product statuses may evolve.

i

The future direction remains flexible and continues to be documented as the project progresses.
