Architecture Overview

The project is still in the process of establishing a fully confirmed architecture. This page documents the high-level direction while keeping technical details clear and provisional where needed.

Documentation pageDetails subject to confirmation

#System context

Prog-Net 3.0 is structured around multiple independent products rather than a single connected launcher. Each product is intended to sit within its own boundaries, with users and products identified clearly once the technical design is confirmed.

i

The architecture should reflect verified relationships and avoid invented integrations.

#Product architecture

Each product concept has its own intended purpose, audience, and technical direction. At this stage, the architecture should be represented conceptually and updated only after the actual implementation and component structure are established.

i

Architecture diagrams are helpful, but they should be based on confirmed design rather than assumptions.
