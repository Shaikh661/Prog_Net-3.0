A project in progress

Built across generations.
Documented with care.

Prog-Net is an academic software development journey that has evolved across three generations. What began as a C-based project gradually expanded into Java Swing applications and has now progressed towards developing independent software products.

Prog-Net 3.0 introduced four product concepts: Handmade Marketplace, MicroJobs, Modern Ticketing System, and Mosque Tracker. As development priorities evolved, Handmade Marketplace and Modern Ticketing System remained active, while MicroJobs and Mosque Tracker were placed on hold.

MISSION & VISIONLearning Through Building

Our goal is to turn classroom knowledge into practical software development experience. Prog-Net encourages experimentation, teamwork, problem-solving, and continuous learning through projects that explore real-world ideas.

LATEST DEVELOPMENTFour Product Concepts

Prog-Net 3.0 introduced four software concepts: Handmade Marketplace, MicroJobs, Modern Ticketing System, and Mosque Tracker. Development currently focuses on Handmade Marketplace and Modern Ticketing System, while MicroJobs and Mosque Tracker remain on hold.
